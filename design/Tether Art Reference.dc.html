02

Planet

light from upper-left · terminator · rim · halo

Layer stack, back to front
  1. Atmospheric halo — radial gradient, r·0.92 → r·1.40, α .28 → 0
  2. Body — radial gradient centred (−0.36r, −0.40r), r·0.06 → r·1.35: light → base → dark
  3. Surface bands — 3–5 clipped ellipses, ry 0.05–0.16r, α .05–.10, drifting ±0.03r on sine
  4. Terminator — radial gradient centred (−0.45r, −0.50r), 0.15r → 2.05r, transparent → rgba(6,6,12,.82)
  5. Rim light — arc(r − lw/2, 0.95π → 1.62π), lw 0.035r, body light colour at α .85
  6. Engraved outline — 1px circle, #D9AC4B at α .22
individuality = seed → palette index, band count, band offsets, radius ±12%. never clone the band layout.

07

Boss world

Build
  • Radius 2× an ordinary planet — the dashed hairline marks standard scale
  • Body gradient #FFC978 → #C4622C → #8E3A22, same upper-left light origin
  • Pulsing halo, two passes of #DD3B2E: r·1.15 → r·1.75 at α .30 → 0, scale ±6% at 0.30 Hz
  • Wide ring — a band, not a line: inner r·1.42, outer r·1.60, brass hairline on both edges, 48 ticks spanning the band, every 6th full width
  • Two dark equatorial bands at α .12 keep the body from reading flat
